Locksmith invoice template

Locksmith work is a trip charge plus parts and labor, and it happens fast. This locksmith invoice template lists the service call, the hardware, and the labor separately so the customer sees exactly what the visit cost.

What to put on a locksmith invoice

  • Service call / dispatch fee
  • Labor — hours or per lock
  • Hardware — locks, keys, cylinders
  • Emergency or after-hours rate
  • Payment terms and due date

Common questions

What does a locksmith put on an invoice?

A service-call or dispatch fee, itemized labor, and itemized hardware. Note the make of the locks or keys where relevant so the customer can verify the parts.

Should I charge extra for lockouts?

Emergency and after-hours callouts often carry a higher rate. State it explicitly on the invoice rather than folding it into the total.

How do I bill a rekey?

Charge per lock plus any new keys or cylinders, each on its own line. A per-lock breakdown is easy for the customer to understand.
