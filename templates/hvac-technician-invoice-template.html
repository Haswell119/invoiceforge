HVAC bills mix a diagnostic call, parts, and labor — and often a maintenance plan. This HVAC invoice template keeps the service call, parts, labor, and any maintenance agreement on separate lines so the customer can follow every charge.

What to put on a hvac technician invoice

  • Service call / diagnostic fee
  • Labor — hours × rate
  • Parts and refrigerant — itemized
  • Maintenance plan or seasonal service
  • Payment terms and due date

Common questions

What should an HVAC technician's invoice include?

A service or diagnostic fee, itemized parts, and labor on separate lines, plus any maintenance plan you bill. List parts with model numbers so the charge is verifiable.

Should I charge a diagnostic fee?

Many contractors charge a diagnostic or service-call fee that is applied toward the repair if the customer proceeds. If you charge one, show it on its own line.

How do I bill a maintenance plan?

Bill the seasonal or annual plan as its own line, or as a separate invoice, and state the period it covers. Keep it distinct from one-off repairs.
