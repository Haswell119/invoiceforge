Moving bills add up fast — hours, crew, truck, packing, and supplies. This moving company invoice template itemizes each so the customer sees the hourly labor, the travel time, and the materials separately.

What to put on a moving company invoice

  • Hourly labor — crew × hours
  • Travel time and fuel
  • Truck and equipment
  • Packing materials and boxes
  • Deposit paid and balance due

Common questions

What should a moving invoice include?

Crew hours, travel time, the truck, and packing materials on separate lines, plus the deposit and the balance due. Itemizing hours by the crew makes the math easy to check.

Do movers bill travel time?

Most charge for travel to and from the job, often at a reduced rate. Show it as its own line so it is not mistaken for on-site labor.

Should I bill packing supplies separately?

Yes — boxes, tape, and wrap are real costs. List them separately so the customer understands why materials are on the bill.
