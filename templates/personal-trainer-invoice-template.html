Training is sold in sessions and packages, which need to be counted clearly. This personal trainer invoice template lists the sessions, any package discount, and extras so the client sees exactly what they are paying for.

What to put on a personal trainer invoice

  • Training sessions — number and rate
  • Package or bundle pricing
  • Program or nutrition plan, if billed
  • Sessions remaining or used
  • Payment terms and due date

Common questions

What goes on a personal trainer invoice?

The number of sessions and the rate, any package discount, and extras like a program or nutrition plan. Show the sessions used or remaining so the client can track the package.

Should I bill per session or per package?

Either works. Billing per package with the sessions itemized gives the client a clear view of value and locks in the commitment.

Do trainers charge for missed sessions?

Many do, with a stated policy such as 24-hour notice. If you charge for a no-show, list it on its own line so it is not confused with a session.
