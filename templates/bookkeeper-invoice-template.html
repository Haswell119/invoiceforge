What to put on a bookkeeper invoice

  • Monthly retainer — scope and period
  • Catch-up or cleanup work
  • Payroll or tax prep, if billed
  • Software pass-through
  • Payment terms and due date

Common questions

What should a bookkeeper invoice include?

The monthly retainer with its scope and period, any one-off cleanup or catch-up work, and any software you pass through, each on its own line.

How do bookkeepers bill?

Most use a monthly retainer for recurring work and bill cleanup or catch-up work separately. State the retainer period so the client knows what it covers.

Do bookkeepers pass through software costs?

If you pay for the client's accounting software, list it at cost on its own line rather than burying it in the retainer.
