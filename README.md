# InvoiceForge — free invoice generator for freelancers & small businesses

Turn a blank page into a client-ready PDF invoice in about a minute. Add your business
details, line items, tax and currency — the totals calculate themselves and your data
never leaves the browser. **No signup, no account, no watermark on your core invoice.**

**Live app:** https://haswell119.github.io/invoiceforge/

## What it does

- Fill in your business name, client, line items, tax rate and currency → get a
  print-ready / save-as-PDF invoice instantly.
- 100% client-side: nothing is uploaded to a server. Your client data stays in your
  browser.
- Free tier produces a complete invoice with a small "Made with InvoiceForge" credit.

## Pro — $19 one-time (lifetime)

- Adds save & reload, so you can keep your business details and template.

## Who it's for

Freelancers, solo consultants, tradespeople and micro-businesses who currently build
invoices in Google Docs or Word. If you just need the invoice done — with a proper
invoice number, line items with quantities and rates, tax and a due date — this is it.

## Run it yourself

This is a static site (single-file HTML + JS). Clone and open `index.html`, or serve the
folder with any static server. No build step, no dependencies, no backend.
